Swift Search

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Swift Search does not collect any personally identifiable information. Anonymous, non-identifiable usage data is collected to help improve the extension. Your searches, highlighted text, and browsing activity are never transmitted anywhere.

1. What we do and do not collect

Swift Search does not collect any personally identifiable information. We never have access to:

  • Your name, email address, or any identifying information
  • Your browsing history or visited URLs
  • The text you highlight or search
  • Any data from the websites you visit
  • Your IP address

Swift Search does collect anonymous, non-identifiable usage analytics to help us understand how the extension is used and improve it over time. See section 3 for full details.

2. Data stored locally on your device

Swift Search stores certain data locally in your browser using Chrome's built-in storage APIs. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. It includes:

  • Your list of saved websites and search configurations
  • Extension preferences and settings (dark mode, layout, etc.)
  • Saved library items and locked highlights
  • Search history (stored locally for your convenience — individual items can be deleted at any time from within the extension)
  • Usage statistics such as search counts (stored locally only and never transmitted anywhere)

If Chrome Sync is enabled on your account, Chrome may sync this data across your own devices. This sync is handled entirely by Google — Swift Search has no involvement in or access to this process. Please refer to Google's privacy policy for more information.

3. Analytics and usage data

Swift Search uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to collect anonymous usage data. This helps us understand which features are used and how to improve the extension. The following events are tracked:

  • menu_shown — when the Swift Search menu appears (includes: length of selected text, number of enabled sites)
  • menu_closed — when the menu is dismissed (includes: how long it was open, whether an action was taken)
  • site_clicked — when you click a search site (includes: site ID and name)
  • feature_used — when you use a feature such as Find, Define, Thesaurus, or AI Summary (includes: feature name, length of text)

No search queries, highlighted text, or personally identifiable information are included in any of these events. Each installation is assigned a randomly generated anonymous client ID (stored locally) to distinguish sessions. This ID cannot be used to identify you personally.

Analytics data is processed by Google Analytics. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information on how Google handles this data.

4. AI features

Swift Search includes optional AI-powered features (AI Summary and AI Define). These use a large language model that runs locally in your browser via WebGPU. The text you send to the AI is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to any external server.

When you first use an AI feature, the extension downloads the model files from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) and model libraries from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). These are one-time downloads cached in your browser. No personal data or text content is included in these requests — they are standard file downloads.

5. Third-party services

When you use Swift Search to search a website, you are redirected to that website's search results. Swift Search does not intercept, log, or store the content of those searches. Any data collection that occurs on those third-party websites is governed by their own privacy policies.

Swift Search makes the following outbound network requests, none of which transmit personal data:

  • Google Favicon Service — used to load website icons in the search menu. Only the domain name is sent; no personal data is included.
  • DuckDuckGo Favicon Service (icons.duckduckgo.com) — used as a fallback favicon source.
  • Dictionary API (api.dictionaryapi.dev) — called when you use the Define feature. Only the looked-up word is sent.
  • Google Search Suggestions (suggestqueries.google.com) — called when search suggestions are enabled in Settings. This feature is off by default and must be enabled manually.
  • Website homepages — when you add a new website, Swift Search may fetch its homepage to automatically detect its search pattern. This is a standard HTTP request containing no personal data.

6. Permissions

Swift Search requests the following Chrome permissions, each used solely to deliver its core functionality:

  • storage — to save your websites, preferences, and settings locally
  • tabs — to open search results in a new tab and detect the active tab when using the keyboard shortcut
  • scripting — to inject the floating search menu into web pages
  • alarms — to keep the extension's background service worker alive so searches feel instant
  • host permissions (<all_urls>) — required so the floating search menu can appear on any webpage you visit
  • offscreen — required to run the local AI model in the background

7. UK and EU users — GDPR and UK GDPR

Swift Search is available globally, including to users in the United Kingdom and European Union. We are committed to complying with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR) where applicable.

No personal data processing

Swift Search does not collect, process, or store any personal data as defined under the GDPR or UK GDPR. Because no personal data is processed by us, no legal basis for processing is required and no data protection obligations in relation to personal data processing arise on our part.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR, individuals have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and port their personal data, and to object to its processing. As Swift Search holds no personal data, there is nothing for us to provide, correct, delete, or transfer. If you believe we hold data about you or have any concerns, please contact us at swiftsearch@outlook.com.

Data transfers

Swift Search does not transfer any personal data outside the UK or European Economic Area (EEA), as no personal data is collected or processed by us.

Data Protection Officer

As we do not process personal data, we are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer under Article 37 of the GDPR or UK GDPR.

Right to lodge a complaint

If you are based in the UK, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. If you are based in the EU, you may contact your local supervisory authority.

8. International users

Swift Search is available worldwide. Because we do not collect or process any personal data, our obligations under most international data protection laws are minimal. Nonetheless, we recognise users' rights under the following frameworks:

United States — California (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), including the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete it, and the right to opt out of its sale. Swift Search does not collect, sell, share, or disclose any personal information about California residents. No opt-out is necessary as no data is collected or sold.

Canada — PIPEDA

Swift Search complies with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). We do not collect personal information from Canadian users. You may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca if you have concerns.

Australia — Privacy Act 1988

Swift Search respects the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988. We do not collect personal information from Australian users. You may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au if you have concerns.

Brazil — LGPD

Swift Search respects the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD). We do not collect or process personal data of Brazilian users. As no data is collected, users' rights under the LGPD are inherently satisfied. For enquiries, contact swiftsearch@outlook.com.

Japan — APPI

Swift Search complies with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI). We do not acquire, use, or retain personal information from users in Japan. For any enquiries, contact swiftsearch@outlook.com.

South Korea — PIPA

Swift Search respects the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). We do not collect, process, or store personal information from users in South Korea. For any enquiries, contact swiftsearch@outlook.com.

Other regions

Regardless of your location, Swift Search does not collect personal data from any user anywhere in the world. If your region has specific data protection legislation not listed above and you have questions about your rights or our practices, please contact us at swiftsearch@outlook.com and we will respond promptly.

9. Children's privacy

Swift Search does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain EU member states). As no data is collected at all, the extension is safe for use by all ages.
